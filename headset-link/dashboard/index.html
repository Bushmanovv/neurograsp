EEG headset

This laptop is the headset — the ESP32 is not in the loop. Each button sends a 5 s .edf snippet to the Raspberry Pi at …, which runs the model and sends its verdict back. Nothing is classified here.

Gate channels (Fp1, Fp2, T3, T4) Other channels

Decoded from the very bytes that went on the wire. Display only: per-channel DC removed, then decimated 200 Hz → 40 Hz through an anti-alias low-pass — without it the 50 Hz mains (≈92% of the raw power here) would fold onto a fake 10 Hz rhythm. So this view is the sub-20 Hz band. The Pi gets the .edf intact and does its own notch, band-pass, CAR and z-score.
